    <div class="rich-copy">
      <p class="body-large">I am a strategy consultant and sociologist, working on how people and organizations change in the age of AI.</p>
      <p>That question has taken me from transformation programs in Central Asia, the Middle East, and Africa to research on inter-firm relationships and household consumption. The settings change; the underlying challenge is familiar: understand how a system behaves, then design a way for people to move it.</p>
      <p>At McKinsey &amp; Company, I worked with banks, natural-resources companies, and governments on strategy and transformation. At HSE University, I studied the social forces behind economic behavior, published research, and taught students how to reason about institutions and evidence.</p>
      <p>I am especially interested in the organizational consequences of AI: not only what new tools can do, but how they alter judgment, coordination, status, and the everyday work of institutions.</p>
    </div>
