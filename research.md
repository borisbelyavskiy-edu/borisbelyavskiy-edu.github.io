      <h2 id="research-profile-title">Human experience, viewed through institutions and relationships.</h2>
      <p class="research-profile-label">Public ORCID biography</p>
      <blockquote class="research-profile-quote">Boris Belyavskiy is a PhD sociologist and MBA candidate at UC Berkeley Haas focused on human experience as a cross-disciplinary topic linking personal development, organizational performance, and institutional design. His work brings together strategy consulting, economic sociology, and political theory to develop practical models of how human assumptions shape growth, productivity, governance, and adaptation across contexts.</blockquote>
